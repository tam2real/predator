# Trade Signals

Generates entry and exit signals based on market conditions.

## Signal Types
- LONG: Higher low confirmed, support held
- SHORT: Lower high confirmed, resistance held
- EXIT: Structure break, trim target hit

## Process
1. Check market structure (HH/HL or LH/LL)
2. Confirm with volume
3. Calculate risk/reward
4. Output signal with confidence
